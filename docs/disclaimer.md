# Disclaimer

New repos nowadays don't even include AI disclaimers but as a member of both the art community and programming community, I feel like it is right for you, the users, to know. As we all know, genAI has been creeping on every aspect of our lives. It has basically taken over programming and is scarily disrupting creative industries. <u>This add-on was made with the heavy use of Claude, and wouldn't have existed without its work.</u>

Everyone has their own tolerance of AI usage. If you feel that this add-on is slop as a result, you are entitled to that opinion. I do not hold any grudge if you feel uncomfortable using this software, nor will I hold it against you. Too many grifters on Twitter/X post about the next "artist replacement" tool, leaving a bad taste for everyone.

My personal take has always been that AI is a tool - it's only as good as its wielder. Every architecture and UX decision was made by me. I made the final decision on what stayed and what didn't, based on my own experience during testing. At its core, this add-on was created with the intent of **giving more opportunities** to both MMD and Blender artists. Cross-platform collabs has always been possible with mmd_tools but this add-on streamlines the entire process. I personally relate to the pain points of trying to fix a vmd motion, one that is locked at 30fps and having to remap its keyframes to my modified MMD rig in Blender.

Of course, there are genuine concerns such as allowing python scripts to run arbitrary code on your machine. This add-on is more high-risk since this is basically a file parser and file system manipulator. To that end, that is why this add-on is FOSS and the files are publicly available so anyone can audit the scripts. As much as possible, I make an effort to review and understand the code that I'm distributing to the public.

To end off this note, I am okay if you don't want to support this addon because of genAI. **My only wish is that you judge this add-on by the goals that it set out to accomplish**. At the end of the day, I set out to make something that could help myself and other artists create more freely. Anyways, that's all from me.
