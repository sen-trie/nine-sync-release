# Frequently Asked Questions

**Will NineSync always be free?**
Yes. NineSync is intended to remain free. This does not override license conditions for third-party models, effects or other assets.

**Do I have to credit NineSync?**
No credit is needed for artwork or animations made with NineSync. If you want to mention or link the project, it helps others find it.

**Can I suggest a feature or report a problem?**
Absolutely. Please share reproducible steps and example files when their licenses permit it. See [Contributors Wanted](contributors.md).

**Was AI used to develop NineSync?**
Yes. AI was used extensively during development. As a Blender animator and a NineSync user, I put significant effort into testing, refining and polishing its usability. The goal is a tool that artists can understand and actually work with.

**Does NineSync replace mmd_tools?**
No. Install and enable [mmd_tools](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mmd-tools/) separately.

**Which render engine should I use for effects?**
Start with **EEVEE**. **Cycles** may work for some imported effects but may need extra shader and transparency adjustments.
