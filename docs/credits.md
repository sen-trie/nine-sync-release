# Credits

NineSync builds on the work of artists and developers who made MMD and Blender workflows possible.

- **[mmd_tools](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mmd-tools/):** A foundational Blender tool for working with MMD models, motions and related data. NineSync uses it as a separate prerequisite for its PMM workflow.
- **[HoovyTools](https://hoovytube.com/hoovytools/):** Inspiration in spirit: helping artists move creative work from a familiar animation application into Blender. HoovyTools focuses on **Source Filmmaker**, not MMD, and is not a NineSync dependency.
- **MMD / MME community and effect authors:** Please respect the original authors' credits, usage rules and redistribution permissions for any effects you use.
