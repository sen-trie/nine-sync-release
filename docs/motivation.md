# Who is this addon for?

## Author

> I'm **sen-trie**, an animator working in Blender. I wanted to collaborate with MMD animators, but moving between the two programs made that collaboration much harder than it needed to be. NineSync grew out of wanting a more practical bridge between those workflows—not to replace MMD, but to let artists use the tools that work best for them.

## For MMD artists

Keep MMD's familiar, approachable animation workflow while using Blender's rendering and scene tools for the final result. You should not have to rebuild every part of a project just to explore a different renderer.

## For Blender artists

NineSync opens up workflows around MMD accessories and supported **MMEffects (MME)** that were traditionally used in MMD. You can experiment with supported effects in Blender, while respecting **each effect creator's license, permissions and redistribution terms**. Compatibility varies by effect, and not every MME technique can be reproduced directly.

## For both

A shared project should not require everyone to abandon their preferred animation tools. NineSync aims to help MMD and Blender artists collaborate on the same production while establishing a more consistent Blender-based rendering pipeline. Results may still need adjustments to lighting, materials and effects.
